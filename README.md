# Evaluating AI for Newsrooms

**I measure whether newsroom AI does what it claims, starting with Arabic.**

My work focuses on building practical evaluation methods for AI-assisted journalism.

I'm particularly interested in the gap between an AI system producing a convincing answer and demonstrating that its output is supported by evidence.

## Current focus

### NAST — AI Evaluation System in Development

NAST is an evaluation system in development, starting with Arabic source and translation fidelity.

The longer-term objective is to evaluate and supervise agentic editorial workflows, including research, verification, translation, and other AI-assisted newsroom tasks.

### Evaluation research and tools

My public work explores:

- Source fidelity and factual consistency
- Agent performance and error detection
- Evaluation datasets and reproducible tests
- False alarms, missed errors, and uncertainty
- Evidence traceability and human editorial review

## What you'll find here

Small, reproducible experiments and practical evaluation tools.

Where results are published, I distinguish demonstrated findings from assumptions, synthetic cases from real-world examples, and AI-generated judgments from independent human review.

## Collaboration

I'm interested in working with newsrooms, fact-checkers, researchers, and AI developers who are evaluating editorial AI systems.

Especially interested in Arabic-language workflows and evidence-based evaluation of autonomous agents.
